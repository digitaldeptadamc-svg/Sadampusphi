Department of Dravyaguna
Sadapuspi
Botanical Name :

  • Catharanthus roseus G.Don

    • Family Name :

  • Apocynaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Nityapushpa

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Sadabahar

  • English – Madagascar Periwinkle

    • Synonyms :

  • Nityakalyani

  • Sadapuspa


    • Habitat :

  • Native to Madagascar and widely cultivated throughout India, grows well in tropical and subtropical climates.


    • External Morphology :

  • Stem: Erect, branched, smooth, green to reddish-green.

  • Leaves: Simple, opposite, elliptic, glossy green with a prominent central vein.

  • Flowers: Attractive, five-petalled, usually pink, rose or white with a central eye.


    • Useful Parts :

  • Whole Plant


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Antidiabetic

  • Antioxidant

  • Antimicrobial


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Prameha (Diabetes): Traditionally used in some regional practices.

  • Vrana (Wounds): Leaf preparations are used externally in traditional practice.
